The problem

Energy waste often goes unseen

Households and institutions lack an intelligent way to analyze usage patterns, predict future needs and find opportunities to cut waste.

High bills

Excessive consumption drives up costs.

Hidden waste

It is hard to tell where energy is being wasted.

Unnoticed spikes

Sudden increases can pass without anyone noticing.

Little guidance

Few tools give personalized ways to reduce usage.

Features

Five capabilities, one dashboard

Designed to move users from looking at past bills to acting on what comes next.

P

Forecasts future consumption from historical data.

D

Interactive daily and monthly usage graphs.

A

R

Recommendations

Suggests ways to reduce energy use.

How it works

From raw data to decisions

A simple five-stage workflow.

Input

Upload historical data or enter usage manually.

Process

Pandas organizes and prepares the data.

Analyze

Machine learning predicts usage and spots unusual patterns.

Display

Graphs, forecasts, alerts and estimated cost.

Advise

Personalized suggestions to reduce wastage.
